## From rice farming to investment
{: #story-title }

Yu grew up in a rice-farming family in Japan and helped with farm work, including raising seedlings, planting rice, crop protection, and harvesting.

He saw that rice is essential to everyday life in Japan, yet many farmers struggle to earn enough from farming alone. This experience shaped his interest in improving the economics of agriculture.

## Professional background

Before pursuing his MBA, Yu spent more than eight years working in asset allocation, quantitative analysis, and financial risk management at a Japanese financial institution.

His responsibilities included market analysis, investment policy development, portfolio management, budget-versus-actual analysis, financial simulations, and recommendations to senior management. He also developed portfolio analysis systems and asset allocation models.

## At Berkeley Haas

At Berkeley Haas, Yu is studying venture capital, startup investing, entrepreneurial finance, AI development, and AI agents. He is particularly interested in how these fields can be applied to agriculture.

## Looking ahead

Yu hopes to work in corporate venture capital and invest in technologies that support agriculture, including robotics, AI, data tools, logistics, financing, and market access.

His long-term goal is to help Japanese farmers build more profitable businesses and contribute to improving Japan’s food self-sufficiency.
